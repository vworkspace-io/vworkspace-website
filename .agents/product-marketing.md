## Product Overview

**Working one-liner:** vWorkspace helps an organization find the operational work slowing it down, then implements AI Employees to handle a bounded workflow while its team remains in control.

**What it does:** vWorkspace combines an organization control panel and system of record with a platform that governs AI Employees, their work, permissions, runs, approvals, and results. Delivery begins with a configured implementation and pilot around one measurable workflow.

**Working product category:** Agentic organization implementation platform.

**Product type:** Configured implementation and pilot backed by a software platform; not currently positioned as self-serve horizontal SaaS.

**Business model and pricing:** To be decided by Sarah. Do not state pricing, packaging, contract terms, support levels, or SLAs.

**Working customer-facing promise:** We find the work slowing your business down, then build AI Employees to handle it—with your team still in control.

**Working platform explanation:** vWorkspace combines an organization control panel with teachable AI Employees that perform operational work with permissions, approvals, and an audit trail.

> **Approval gate:** The public category, headline, customer-facing promise, and final platform explanation require Sarah's approval.

## Target Audience

**Confirmed target condition:** An organization or business has identifiable operational friction and is willing to scope one bounded, measurable workflow for a pilot.

**Company size, industry, and geography:** Not yet validated. Do not reuse the current website's company-size or sector claims as new ICP facts.

**Buyer and champion roles:** Not yet validated. Working discovery should identify:
- the person accountable for the affected business outcome;
- the operator or team that performs the workflow today;
- the owner of systems, data, permissions, and risk;
- the financial decision-maker for a pilot.

These are buying-role placeholders, not public persona claims.

**Primary use case:** Discover, implement, and evaluate one operational workflow that an AI Employee can perform within explicit human and system boundaries.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Find where operational work is delayed, repetitive, or dependent on avoidable handoffs.
- Turn one suitable workflow into governed work for an AI Employee.
- Keep permissions, approvals, runs, and results visible to accountable people.
- Measure the pilot against a baseline and decide what should be repeated, changed, or stopped.

**Anti-persona / poor fit:**
- Teams seeking an unbounded autonomous system with no accountable human owner.
- Buyers requiring unsupported production runtimes or unverified integrations on day one.
- Prospects unwilling to define a workflow, baseline, success measure, or human/AI boundary.

## Problems and Switching Dynamics

**Core problem:** Important operational work can be slowed by repeated coordination, manual handoffs, fragmented context, and tasks that depend on scarce people.

**Push:** A specific workflow creates visible delay, rework, backlog, or coordination overhead.

**Pull:** A bounded implementation offers a practical way to put an AI Employee to work while preserving control and accountability.

**Habit:** Teams continue using familiar manual processes because replacing an entire system or adopting a broad AI platform feels risky.

**Anxiety:** Loss of control, unclear accountability, unsafe access, unpredictable behavior, and uncertain business value.

**Current evidence gap:** These are working problem hypotheses. No verbatim customer research or quantified cost has been approved for citation.

## Differentiation

**Working differentiators:**
- Begins with operational friction and a measurable workflow, not a generic feature rollout.
- Combines implementation work with a governance platform for AI Employees.
- Makes permissions, runs, approvals, and results part of the operating model.
- Uses a teachable execution runtime behind an adapter rather than making the runtime the product.
- Turns repeated, evidenced implementation patterns into templates over time.

**Important constraint:** Present these as characteristics of the approach, not as unique or superior claims, until competitive research supports that language.

## Delivery Offer: Agentic Organization Pilot

The pilot is the bounded path into the product:

1. Discover operational friction with the customer.
2. Select one workflow with a named owner and measurable baseline.
3. Define the human/AI boundary, permissions, approvals, and exception path.
4. Configure and implement the AI Employee and supporting workflow.
5. Run the workflow, capture results, and compare them with the baseline.
6. Decide whether to stop, refine, repeat, or turn the pattern into a reusable template.

Do not promise a duration, fixed scope, price, ROI, implementation speed, support level, or guaranteed result until those terms are approved.

## Claim Governance

- **Working:** May be used in internal drafts. Public use still requires Sarah's approval.

- **Evidence required:** Needs a documented source, measurement, and publication permission.
- **Reserved:** Sarah must decide the wording or commercial position.
- **Do not claim:** Unsupported or contrary to the current product boundary.

## Brand Voice

**Style:**
- Lead with the customer's workflow and desired operating outcome.
- Prefer plain language and short, concrete sentences.
- Explain control alongside capability.

**Personality:** Practical, precise, calm, transparent, and collaborative.

**Do not sound:** Futuristic, grandiose, fear-driven, anti-human, or certain beyond the evidence.
